What it already knew

    It notices what you’d lose track of.

    A reminder buried in a text. A deadline in a school newsletter. Loose Ends reads them on your Mac and turns each one into a loose end, with the reason it matters written on it. You never have to write it down.

    Your life stays on your Mac.

    Loose Ends is self-hosted. The engine runs on your own Mac and reads the copies of your mail and messages that are already there. We never see what’s on your phone or your Mac, and there’s no account to create.

    Your iPhone talks to your Mac, not to us. The AI works under your own Google Gemini key.

    Coming soon.

    Loose Ends isn’t available to download right now. When it’s back, you’ll need a Mac that stays on, an iPhone, and a free Google Gemini API key, and about ten minutes to set it up.
