Every “I’ll get back to you,” handed back.

Loose Ends reads the messages and mail already on your Mac, notices what you promised, works on it while you don’t, and hands it back to your phone. You never type a task.

It was already in the conversation.

Most of what you owe people was said out loud: in a text, in a reply, in a thread you meant to come back to. Loose Ends reads those on your Mac and turns them into loose ends, each with the reason it surfaced written on it.

There is no Loose Ends cloud.

The engine runs on your Mac and reads the copies of your mail and messages that are already there. The AI works under your own Gemini API key. Your iPhone talks to your Mac, not to us.

The one hosted piece is a push relay. This is everything it accepts:

POST /v1/push
{
  "device_tokens": ["9f3c…e1a4"],
  "level":         "active",
  "collapse_id":   "t_8412",
  "thread_id":     "t_8412",
  "finding_id":    "f_2210"
}

A device token and opaque ids. No field can hold a word from your messages, so the relay can only say “wake up.” Your phone gets the words from your Mac.

About ten minutes to set up.

You need a Mac that stays on, an iPhone, and a free Google Gemini API key. The setup page walks you through getting one. Full prerequisites and the honest edges are in the README.
